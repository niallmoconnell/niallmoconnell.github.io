Back up or restore birthdays

Open Settings in Bdays. Choose Save a copy to store a birthday file somewhere safe, or Restore from file to bring a saved list back.

Reminders aren’t appearing

Open Bdays Settings and enable reminders. You can also check iPhone Settings → Notifications → Bdays and confirm that notifications are allowed.

Deleting your data

Birthday records can be removed inside the app. Uninstalling Bdays removes its locally stored data from that device, so save a copy first if you may want it later.
