Bdays

PRIVACY POLICY

Your birthdays stay yours.

Effective 11 September 2026

Overview

Bdays is designed to work without an account or cloud database. The birthday and contact information you enter is stored locally on your device.

Information stored by Bdays

Bdays can store names, nicknames, birthdays, phone numbers, preferred messaging apps, and notes that you choose to enter. This information remains in the app’s local storage on your device.

Information we collect

We do not collect, receive, sell, or use your birthday records, contact information, messages, or usage data. Bdays does not include advertising or third-party analytics.

Reminders

If you enable reminders, Bdays schedules notifications locally through your device. You can disable notifications at any time in Bdays or in your device settings.

Backups and imports

You may choose to export a birthday file or import one. Exported files are sent only to the location or service you select through the device sharing interface. Their storage and handling are then governed by that service’s privacy practices.

Messaging apps

When you choose a messaging option, Bdays opens the selected third-party app with your chosen message where supported. Bdays does not send the message itself. Your use of that app is governed by its own privacy policy.

Deleting your information

You can delete individual birthday records within Bdays. Uninstalling the app removes its locally stored data from that device. Exported backup files must be deleted separately wherever you saved them.

Children’s privacy

Bdays is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If Bdays’ data practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes take effect.
